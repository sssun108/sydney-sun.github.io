Serenity Pond

Design and Build

2022
Solo Designer
Completed

Serenity Pond is a design + build addition to a back yard of a private residence in Maryland. Materials consist of cast in place concrete, concrete masonry units, and mortar. The pond brings elements of nature closer to the residents, creating a space for peaceful reflection.

Construction completed in 2022.
